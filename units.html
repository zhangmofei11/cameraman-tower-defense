Unit Guide

Cameraman

The basic ranged unit. Good starting choice for new players.

  • Damage: Medium
  • Attack Speed: Fast
  • Range: Long
  • Cost: Low

TV Man

Aerial attacks make this unit great against flying enemies.

  • Damage: Medium-High
  • Attack Speed: Medium
  • Range: Very Long
  • Special: Can hit aerial enemies

Speakerman

High damage dealer with area-of-effect attacks.

  • Damage: Very High
  • Attack Speed: Slow
  • Range: Medium
  • Special: Splash damage

Titan Cameraman

The ultimate unit. Massive damage but expensive to summon.

  • Damage: Extreme
  • Attack Speed: Medium
  • Range: Long
  • Cost: Very High

Upgraded Units

Some units have upgraded versions with better stats:

  • Upgraded Titan Speakerman - Enhanced version with higher damage
  • Elite Cameraman - Improved attack speed and range
  • Golden TV Man - Premium version with special effects
