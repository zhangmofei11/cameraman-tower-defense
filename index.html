ABOUT THE GAME

Cameraman Tower Defense is a Roblox tower defense game based on the popular Skibidi Toilet internet meme series. Players deploy Cameramen and various units to defend against waves of invading toilets. The game features multiple characters from the Skibidi Toilet universe including Cameraman, Titan Cameraman, Speakerman, and TV Man.
