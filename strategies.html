📹 Cameraman Tower Defense

Strategies

Early Game Tips

  • Focus on Cameraman - He's cost-effective and reliable
  • Save coins - Don't spend everything on the first waves
  • Learn enemy patterns - Some toilets are faster or have more HP
  • Position units well - Place ranged units in the back

Mid Game Strategy

  • Add TV Man - Essential for aerial enemies
  • Upgrade key units - Focus on your best performers
  • Watch for mini-bosses - They have more HP and require focus fire
  • Balance offense and defense - Don't neglect either

Late Game Waves

  • Save for Titan Cameraman - He's worth the investment
  • Stack damage dealers - Late waves have high HP enemies
  • Use Speakerman wisely - His splash damage is crucial
  • Coordinate with team - Communication is key in co-op

Co-op Tips

  • Divide responsibilities - One defends left, one defends right
  • Share units strategically - Cover each other's weaknesses
  • Call out enemy positions - Help teammates target threats
  • Combine unit types - Mix ranged and splash damage units
